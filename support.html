Support Us

Help keep East Perth vibrant, connected and well cared for
The East Perth Community Group (EPCG) is committed to the growth and development of the East Perth area. We support, manage and instigate projects for a community where residents live rich, connected lives, local businesses are well patronised, and visitors find a warm welcome in our vibrant riverside village. Everything we do is run by local volunteers - here are two easy ways you can back it.

Become a member

Membership is the best way to support the EPCG and have your say. Financial members also go into our members-only draws and get the inside word on everything happening in East Perth.

Donate with Containers for Change

Recycling your eligible drink containers? Please donate the 10c refund to the EPCG - it turns your empties into real community support, at no cost to you.

Tell us about your event

Running something in East Perth - a market stall, a working bee, a club night, a fundraiser? Send it to us and we'll consider it for the Hub and the newsletter. Community events are the point of all this, so please don't be shy.

Prefer email? Write to secretary.eastperthcommunity@gmail.com. We're all volunteers, so give us a few days.
