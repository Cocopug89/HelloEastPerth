City of Perth 3-bin kerbside collection, April to December 2026. FOGO goes out every week; recycling and general waste alternate on fortnightly weeks. Dates are computed from the standard fortnightly cycle - check the City's official calendar for public-holiday changes (especially late December).
Getting your bins out
Day collection: your bin must be at its service location by 6am. Night collection: by 8pm. Exception - some West Perth night services: by 4pm. Put bins out no later than the time above.
Faded sticker? Contact the City on (08) 9461 3333.
Full details & official calendar: https://perth.wa.gov.au/residents/rubbish-waste-and-recycling

Approved fireworks events and close-proximity notifications across the Perth metropolitan area, from the WorkSafe WA schedule. Useful if you are wondering what the bangs were, or planning around a nervous dog.
This is a safety schedule, not an events calendar. WorkSafe WA publishes it for guidance only and states: Start times and duration may vary from that indicated in the schedule. It also states: While the department regulates the safe use of fireworks, it does not manage the fireworks displays. For specific information relating to any individual display, please contact the event organiser directly. Treat every time below as approximate and check with the organiser before relying on it.
